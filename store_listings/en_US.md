## 3. Full Description [Max: 4000 Characters]
```text
Experience the ultimate edition of Nine Men's Morris, one of the world’s oldest and most captivating board games! Morris World brings together 3 Men's Morris, 9 Men's Morris, and 12 Men's Morris (Morabaraba) in one polished, competitive, and feature-rich package.

Whether you want to challenge players around the globe, train against smart AI, solve tactical puzzles, or pass-and-play with friends and family, this is the definitive strategy experience you've been waiting for!

🎯 3 GAMES IN ONE
• 3 Men's Morris: Fast, furious, and fun. Perfect for quick mental warmups.
• 9 Men's Morris (The Mill Game): The timeless classic played for over 3,000 years across empires and civilizations.
• 12 Men's Morris (Morabaraba): Featuring diagonal lines and 12 pieces for deeper combinations and high-stakes maneuvers!

⚔️ MULTIPLE GAME MODES
• Online Multiplayer: Instant matchmaking with players worldwide or private rooms to challenge your friends.
• Tactics Arena: Put your mind to the test with hand-crafted tactical puzzles! Learn critical endgame traps, escape stalemates, and master one-move mills.
• Adaptive AI Opponent: 4 finely balanced difficulty tiers — Apprentice, Tactician, Mill Master, and Grandmaster.
• Local Pass & Play: Play head-to-head on a single device with a friend or family member anytime, anywhere.

✨ PREMIUM FEATURES
• Beautiful Themes: Customize your board and stones with authentic Wood, Royal Marble, Neon Cyber, and Clean Slate styles.
• Global Leagues & Rankings: Earn rating points, rise through the divisions, and claim the Grandmaster crown.
• 100% Offline Capability: Play full games against AI or local friends without needing Wi-Fi or cellular data.
• Immersive Haptics & Audio: Tactile feedback on every placement, mill completion, and capture with relaxing background melodies.
• Authentic Rules: Full support for official mill locking, stalemate prevention, and 3-piece flying phase.

📜 HOW TO PLAY
1. Placement Phase: Players take turns placing stones onto vacant board intersections.
2. Movement Phase: Slide your stones along adjacent lines to form a row of three (a "Mill").
3. Capture: When you form a mill, remove one of your opponent's pieces that isn't part of an existing mill.
4. Flying Phase: When reduced to 3 pieces, your stones can fly to any vacant point on the entire board!
5. Win by reducing your opponent to fewer than 3 pieces or leaving them with zero legal moves.

Train your mind, outsmart your rivals, and master the board. Download Morris World today for free!
```
